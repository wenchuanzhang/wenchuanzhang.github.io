About Me

I am currently pursuing a joint Ph.D. degree in Computer Science at Beijing Normal-Hong Kong Baptist University (BNBU), Zhuhai, China, and Hong Kong Baptist University (HKBU), Hong Kong. My Ph.D. advisor is Dr. Wentao Fan.

My research interests include machine learning, deep learning, graph neural networks, graph representation learning, geometric deep learning, and generative modeling. My recent research focuses on geometric representation learning, graph structure learning, link prediction, graph clustering, hyperspherical learning, diffusion models, and spiking generative models.
